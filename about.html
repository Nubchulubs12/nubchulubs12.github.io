Mortgage Calculator is a free online tool built to help visitors estimate monthly mortgage payments based on loan amount, interest rate, loan term, and optional down payment. It is designed for educational and informational use so users can better understand how mortgage costs work.

What this website helps you do

This calculator can help you compare payment scenarios before speaking with a lender. For example, you can test how a larger down payment, a shorter loan term, or a lower interest rate may affect your estimated monthly payment and total interest over time.

How it works

  • Loan Amount: The amount being financed.
  • Interest Rate: The annual rate used to estimate the cost of borrowing.
  • Loan Term: The number of years over which the loan is repaid.
  • Down Payment: An optional upfront amount that reduces the principal financed.

What is included in the estimate

The calculator estimates principal and interest for a fixed-rate mortgage. It also shows the estimated total interest paid over the life of the loan and the total amount paid.

What is not included

This calculator does not include property taxes, homeowners insurance, PMI, HOA dues, lender fees, closing costs, escrow adjustments, or other location-specific and lender-specific charges. Because of that, actual monthly mortgage payments may be higher than the estimate shown on this site.

Who this tool is for

This website is intended for home buyers, homeowners, borrowers exploring refinance options, and anyone who wants a quick mortgage estimate before applying with a lender.

Important note

This website is not a lender and does not provide financial, legal, or tax advice. Always confirm final numbers with a qualified mortgage professional or financial advisor.
